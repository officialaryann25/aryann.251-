❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery?
Yes! We offer home delivery across Delhi NCR. Orders above ₹500 get FREE delivery. We ensure our ice creams are delivered in insulated packaging to maintain freshness and temperature.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major payment methods including credit/debit cards, UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm), net banking, and cash on delivery. All online payments are 100% secure.
Are your ice creams made with natural ingredients?
Absolutely! We use only real fruits, premium dairy, and natural flavors. We never use artificial preservatives or colors. Every ingredient is carefully sourced to ensure the highest quality.
Can I place bulk orders for events?
Yes! We cater to parties, weddings, corporate events, and other occasions. Contact Mahi at +91 8750141860 or Priyanshi at +91 8368573180 for bulk order enquiries. Special discounts available for orders above ₹5000.
Do you have any vegan or sugar-free options?
Yes! We offer several vegan sorbets and plant-based options. We also have sugar-free variants for health-conscious customers. Check our menu for options labeled "Vegan" or "Sugar-Free".
How can I track my order?
Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive an SMS and email with tracking details. You can also call us directly for real-time order status. Our delivery partners provide live location tracking.
