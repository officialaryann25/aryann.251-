The Sweet Story of IceWorld

Born from a simple love of ice cream and a dream to spread sweetness, IceWorld was founded in 2019 by two passionate entrepreneurs — Mahi Sehrawat and Priyanshi — right in the heart of Delhi NCR.

What started as a small experiment in a home kitchen — mixing classic Indian flavors with modern Western techniques — quickly grew into one of the most loved ice cream brands in the region. Our signature Gulab Jamun Sundae and Mango Kulfi became instant hits!

Today, IceWorld boasts over 50 unique flavors, serves 10,000+ happy customers, and continues to innovate with new seasonal specials and premium offerings. Every scoop we make is a promise of quality, freshness, and pure joy.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To spread happiness one scoop at a time by crafting the finest, most innovative ice creams using natural ingredients, while making every customer feel special.

  • Deliver joy through premium frozen treats
  • Use only natural, high-quality ingredients
  • Innovate with Indian and global flavors
  • Build a community of ice cream lovers
🌟

Our Vision

To become India's most beloved ice cream brand — a household name synonymous with quality, creativity, and sweetness across every corner of the nation.

  • Expand to 50+ cities across India
  • Launch 100+ flavor varieties
  • Create a zero-waste production process
  • Empower local farmers and suppliers

📅 Journey

Our Timeline

The milestones that shaped IceWorld's sweet journey

🌱
2019 - The Beginning

IceWorld is Founded

Mahi Sehrawat and Priyanshi launch IceWorld from their home kitchen in Delhi. The first batch of Gulab Jamun Sundae gets an overwhelming response.

🏪
2020 - First Store

Opening Our First Parlour

Despite the pandemic, IceWorld opens its first physical store in Gurugram. The unique flavors attract lines of customers every day.

🚀
2021 - Digital Expansion

Going Online

IceWorld launches its e-commerce platform and delivery service, reaching customers across Delhi NCR. Online orders surge by 300% in 6 months.

🏆
2022 - Recognition

Award Winning Brand

IceWorld wins the "Best Dessert Brand" award at the Delhi Food Expo. Featured in multiple food magazines and social media influencer reviews.

💎
2023 - Premium Launch

Premium Collection

IceWorld launches its premium collection featuring exotic ingredients. The Triple Berry Parfait and Alphonso Mango Gelato become instant bestsellers.

🌟
2024 - Present

10,000+ Happy Customers

IceWorld proudly serves over 10,000 happy customers with 50+ unique flavors. Expansion plans for Mumbai and Bangalore are underway!
