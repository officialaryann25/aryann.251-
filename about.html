How It All Began 🌟

A dream, a scoop, and a whole lot of love

In 2009, two passionate food lovers — Mahi Sehrawat and Priyanshi — set out on a delicious mission: to bring handcrafted, honest, and joyful ice cream to every corner of the city. Starting from a cozy 200 sq ft parlour with nothing but family recipes, boundless passion, and eight carefully crafted flavours, IceWorld was born. Over the years, their unwavering commitment to quality, creativity, and community has transformed that humble little shop into one of the city's most loved dessert destinations. Every scoop you enjoy today carries the warmth of that original kitchen and the dedication of an entire team that believes ice cream is not just food — it's a feeling.

2009

🌱 The Sweet Beginning

Mahi Sehrawat and Priyanshi opened the first IceWorld parlour in a cozy 200 sq ft space with just 8 flavours and a dream to bring handcrafted happiness to every customer. Word spread quickly through the neighbourhood, and queues formed outside their tiny shop within the first week.

2012

🥇 First Award

IceWorld won the 'Best Artisan Ice Cream' award at the Delhi Food Festival. We expanded our menu to 25 flavours and hired our first full-time pastry chef. This recognition put IceWorld firmly on the city's culinary map and inspired the team to push creative boundaries even further.

2015

🏙️ Expansion Time

Opened three new locations across the city. Introduced our signature Belgian Waffle Stack and the now-legendary Chocolate Fudge Sundae. Each new outlet was designed with a unique theme while maintaining the warm, welcoming atmosphere that our original customers had come to love.

2018

📱 Going Online

Launched our online ordering platform and delivery service. Within months, we were delivering 500+ orders per day to happy customers. The platform also allowed us to introduce limited-edition flavours and special seasonal boxes that customers could pre-order online.

2021

🎉 100K Milestone

Celebrated serving our 100,000th customer! Expanded into gelato and vegan sorbet categories to cater to all dietary preferences. Launched our IceWorld Loyalty Programme, allowing our most dedicated fans to earn rewards with every single scoop they enjoy.

2024

✨ Present Day

Today IceWorld offers 50+ handcrafted flavours, a loyalty programme with 50,000 members, and continues to innovate with new seasonal specials every week. We are prouder than ever of the community we have built and remain committed to delivering joy, one scoop at a time.

Our Mission & Values

Everything we do is guided by four core principles

🏆

Quality First

We use only the finest natural ingredients sourced from trusted farms. No artificial colours, no preservatives — just pure, honest goodness in every single scoop we serve. Quality is not a choice for us; it is a promise we make to every customer.

🌿

Always Fresh

Every batch is made fresh daily. We never serve yesterday's ice cream. Our commitment to freshness is non-negotiable. Our production team begins work at 5am every morning so that fresh batches are ready when we open at 10am.

😊

Joy in Every Scoop

We believe ice cream should make you smile. Every product is designed to create a moment of pure joy. From our whimsical flavour names to our cheerful store décor, every element of the IceWorld experience is crafted to bring happiness.

🤝

Community Love

We're deeply connected to our local community. We source locally, hire locally, and give back through regular community events. IceWorld is not just a brand — it's a neighbour, a friend, and a celebration of the city we call home.

Only the Best Ingredients 🌿

We never compromise on what goes into your scoop

🥛

Farm-Fresh Dairy

All our dairy comes from certified organic farms in Maharashtra. Rich, creamy, and full of natural goodness. We visit our farm partners quarterly to ensure animal welfare and sustainable farming practices are upheld to the highest standards.

🍓

Seasonal Fruits

Fruits are sourced fresh from local farmers at peak season. From Alphonso mangoes to Mahabaleshwar strawberries — only the ripest, most flavourful fruits make it into our parlour. We never use fruit concentrates or artificial flavour compounds.

🍫

Premium Chocolate

We use Belgian Callebaut chocolate for our chocolate-based products. Rich, complex, and absolutely indulgent. Each batch is carefully tempered by our pastry chefs to achieve the perfect glossy finish and satisfying snap in our chocolate-coated treats.

🌰

Natural Nuts

Hand-selected pistachios, hazelnuts, and pecans roasted in-house for maximum flavour and freshness. We source our nuts from certified suppliers and roast small batches daily to ensure optimal crunch and aroma in every bowl and cone we serve.

Giving Back to Our Community 🤝

Because a great business is built on the strength of the community around it

School Treats Programme

Every month, we donate complimentary ice cream to 5 local schools as part of our commitment to spreading joy among children. We have reached over 10,000 young smiles through this initiative.

Eco-Friendly Packaging

100% biodegradable cups, spoons, and packaging across all our outlets and delivery orders. We eliminated single-use plastics entirely in 2020 and continue to explore even more sustainable alternatives every year.
