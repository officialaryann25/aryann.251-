📖 Our Story

Born from a Love of Ice Cream

IceWorld began in 2014 as a small cart at the corner of a busy Delhi street. Founded by Mahi Sehrawat and Priyanshi, two childhood friends who shared an unbreakable bond over their love for ice cream, the dream was simple – to share that joy with the world.

What started with 5 handcrafted flavors sold from a modest cart has grown into a beloved premium ice cream brand serving over 5,000 happy customers across Delhi NCR. Today, IceWorld is synonymous with quality, creativity, and the pure happiness of a perfectly crafted scoop.

We believe ice cream isn't just a dessert — it's an emotion. Every flavor we create tells a story, carries a memory, and brings people together. That's why we pour our hearts into every batch we make.

Our Ingredients

We believe what goes in matters just as much as what comes out. Only the finest, freshest ingredients for you.

🥛

Fresh Farm Milk

Sourced daily from certified local dairy farms, our milk is pure, full-fat, and free of additives for the creamiest texture.

🍓

Real Fruits

We use only real, fresh, seasonal fruits – no artificial flavors or syrups. Every berry, mango, and strawberry is picked at peak ripeness.

🌼

Saffron & Spices

Premium Kashmiri saffron, cardamom and natural spices add authentic Indian flavor to our signature recipes.

🍫

Belgian Chocolate

Only single-origin Belgian chocolate goes into our chocolate creations – rich, velvety, and irresistibly indulgent.

🌰

Premium Nuts

Hand-selected pistachios, almonds, cashews, and walnuts sourced from trusted farms for the perfect crunch.

🍯

Natural Sweeteners

We use pure cane sugar, organic honey, and natural jaggery – no high-fructose corn syrup or artificial sweeteners.

🎯 Our Mission

Spreading Joy, One Scoop at a Time

Our mission is to create moments of pure happiness through exceptional ice cream. We are committed to using only the finest natural ingredients, maintaining the highest standards of hygiene, and continuously innovating to bring new and exciting flavors to our customers.

We believe that great ice cream has the power to make any moment memorable – whether it's celebrating a birthday, cooling off on a hot day, or simply treating yourself because you deserve it.
