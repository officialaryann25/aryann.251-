The Beginning

Our Sweet Journey

IceWorld was born from a simple, heartfelt belief — that ice cream is more than a dessert. It's a feeling. It's the joy of a summer afternoon, the celebration of a special moment, the comfort on a difficult day.

Founded by MAHI SEHRAWAT and Priyanshi, IceWorld started as a small home kitchen experiment. Armed with family recipes, premium ingredients, and an unquenchable passion for crafting the perfect scoop, they set out to create something truly extraordinary.

What began as a weekend hobby quickly grew into a full-fledged brand, loved by thousands across the city. Today, IceWorld stands as a symbol of quality, creativity, and above all — love. Every tub, every cone, every cake is handcrafted with the same care and dedication that started it all.
