Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer nationwide delivery. Orders over $30 qualify for free delivery. Standard delivery takes 1–3 business days.

Can I customise a cake for a special occasion?

Absolutely! Contact us at least 5 days in advance with your requirements. We offer custom flavours, sizes and decorations.

Are your products suitable for vegans?

We offer several vegan-friendly options made with oat milk and coconut cream. Look for the vegan tag on our product pages.

Do you cater for allergies?

Please review the allergen information on each product page. Contact us directly if you have specific allergy concerns.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also log into your account to track orders.

What is your return policy?

Due to the perishable nature of our products, we can't accept returns. However, if you're not satisfied, contact us within 24 hours and we'll make it right.
