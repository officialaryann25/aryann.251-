Our Story

Crafting Joy, One Scoop at a Time

IceWorld was born in the summer of 2010 when our founder, Emma Clarke, couldn't find ice cream that reminded her of her grandmother's homemade recipes. Armed with an old ice cream maker and a passion for real ingredients, she started experimenting in her kitchen.

What began as a small stall at the local farmer's market quickly grew into a beloved neighbourhood shop. Today, IceWorld serves over 10,000 happy customers every month, but our commitment to quality and artisan craftsmanship hasn't changed one bit.

Every scoop we serve is made from scratch, using locally sourced milk and cream, seasonal fruits, and absolutely no artificial colours or flavours. We believe ice cream should taste exactly the way nature intended – pure, simple, and absolutely delicious.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Natural Ingredients

We use only the finest natural ingredients, free from artificial colours, flavours and preservatives. Real food, real taste.

Handcrafted with Love

Every batch is made in small quantities using traditional churning methods to ensure the perfect texture and taste.

Sustainable Practices

We partner with local, eco-friendly farms and use 100% compostable packaging to reduce our environmental footprint.

Community First

We give back to our community by supporting local farmers, sponsoring school events, and donating a scoop for every purchase.
