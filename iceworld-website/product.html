About Strawberry Dream

Our Strawberry Dream ice cream is made using a traditional churning method that creates an ultra-smooth, creamy texture. We source our strawberries from local farms during peak season to ensure maximum flavour.

  • Made with real, fresh strawberries
  • No artificial colours or flavours
  • Gluten-free base
  • Suitable for vegetarians
  • Available as single, double, or triple scoop, or as a pint to take home

Ingredients

Full-cream milk, fresh strawberries (25%), cane sugar, cream, egg yolk, vanilla extract, strawberry purée, stabiliser (guar gum).

Allergen Information

Contains: Milk, Eggs. May contain traces of nuts, wheat, and soy. Manufactured in a facility that handles all major allergens.
