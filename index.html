💛 Our Story

Crafted with Love Since 2014

IceWorld was born from a simple dream – to bring happiness through the most delightful desserts. Founded in 2014 by two passionate food lovers, we started with just 5 flavors and a tiny cart. Today, we're proud to serve 100+ premium flavors to over 5,000 satisfied customers.

Every scoop we serve is handcrafted using fresh, locally sourced ingredients with no artificial preservatives. From our signature Kesar Pista to globally inspired Tiramisu Dream – there's a flavor for every mood!
