      Got Questions?

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know about IceWorld, our products, and how we operate.

      We use only the finest natural ingredients: fresh whole milk and cream from local family farms, real fruit purées and pieces, natural flavour extracts, cane sugar, and free-range eggs. We never use artificial colours, flavours, or preservatives. All allergen information is clearly displayed on each product page.

      Absolutely! Our Sorbet & Vegan range is 100% dairy-free and vegan-certified. We use coconut milk, oat milk, and cashew milk as bases for our vegan ice cream options. These are clearly labelled with a 🌿 Vegan badge on the menu. We also take cross-contamination seriously and prepare vegan products in a dedicated area.

      We offer delivery within a 10-mile radius of our store in Manhattan. Our target delivery time is 30 minutes from order placement. Orders are delivered in insulated cool bags to maintain perfect temperature. If your order takes longer than 45 minutes, we'll refund your delivery fee. Delivery is free on orders over $30.

      Yes! For in-store orders you can mix and match scoops, choose your cone or cup, and select from 20+ toppings. For custom ice cream cakes and catering orders, please contact us at least 48 hours in advance via our contact form or by phone. We love creating bespoke flavour combinations for birthdays, weddings, and corporate events.

      Every dollar you spend earns 1 IcePoint. Once you've collected 50 IcePoints, you receive a $5 reward voucher automatically. Members also get early access to new flavours, exclusive birthday offers, and double points every Tuesday. Sign up for free via the Register tab in our account section.

      Yes, catering is one of our specialities! We can bring our mobile ice cream cart to your event, provide pre-packed artisan cups, or create a stunning custom ice cream cake centrepiece. We've catered for everything from intimate birthday parties to large-scale corporate events for 500+ guests. Contact us for a tailored quote.

      Promo codes can be entered at the checkout stage. Click on the "Have a promo code?" link on the checkout page, type your code, and click Apply. Valid codes are automatically deducted from your total. Each code can only be used once per account unless otherwise stated. Subscribe to our newsletter to receive exclusive promo codes first.

      Our ice creams may contain milk, eggs, gluten (waffle cones/crepes), nuts (pistachio, hazelnut, almond flavours), peanuts, soy, and other allergens. Each product page lists full ingredient and allergen information. Our kitchen handles all major allergens, so we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment. Please speak with our staff if you have severe allergies.

      Our Story

      Passion in Every Scoop

      IceWorld was founded in 2009 by the Mercer family with a simple dream: to create the most delicious, freshest, and most imaginative ice cream in the world. Starting with a small cart at the local farmers' market, we've grown into a beloved destination for ice cream lovers of all ages.

      We source our milk and cream from local family farms, use only natural flavourings, and never add artificial preservatives. Every batch is hand-churned in our open kitchen, where you can watch the magic happen.

      No artificial colours or preservatives
